🎨 Appendix: Sample Art-Based Therapy Workshop Outline

Workshop Theme: "Painting Our Resilience: Expressing Past, Present, and Hope" (90 Minutes)

Step 1: Grounding and Connection (15 min)

Guided deep breathing and a brief introduction to art as a language. Participants share their favorite colors and what they symbolize (a non-verbal check-in).

Step 2: The Landscape of Emotion (40 min)

Participants create three distinct abstract sections on a single piece of paper using tempera or pastels:

  • **Left:** Colors/shapes representing the past (grief, memory, or strength).
  • **Center:** Colors/forms representing the present feeling (anxiety, calm, or complexity).
  • **Right:** Colors/patterns representing hope, future community, or a safe place.

Step 3: Finding the Bridge (20 min)

Participants are encouraged to find lines or shapes that organically **connect** the three distinct sections, turning the isolated feelings into a cohesive landscape. This models emotional integration and moving forward.

Step 4: Reflection and Conclusion (15 min)

Voluntary sharing of the artwork, focusing not on technical skill but on the feelings evoked during the process. The facilitator emphasizes that resilience is the process of connecting difficult emotions with hope.

🌟 Holistic Impact: Sustainability and Mental Health Benefits

Social Sustainability 🫂

The collaborative painting process fosters **social cohesion** and trust, turning historical grief into shared, positive action. The building becomes a neutral, beautiful hub for community gathering.

Economic Sustainability 💰

The use of durable, high-quality paint reduces long-term maintenance costs for the community. The revitalized space enhances **civic pride** and supports local economic engagement.

Environmental Sustainability 🌳

Using **low-VOC (Volatile Organic Compound) exterior paint** minimizes environmental toxins while providing long-term structural protection, ensuring a sustainable investment in community infrastructure.

🧠 Mental Health Benefits of Volunteerism

For the volunteer team, the project provides a profound sense of **purpose and self-efficacy**, reducing stress and fostering deep social connection through shared, meaningful labor and immersion in a new culture.
